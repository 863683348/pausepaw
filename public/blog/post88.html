Android Digital Wellbeing 完整说明

2026-11-03

每一项设置都讲清楚，再补上多数指南略过的三条限制。

每台 Android 手机都自带 android digital wellbeing（数字健康），但多数人只打开过一次，看一眼柱状图就再也没回来。有点可惜：里面四项控制能做到的事，第三方 App 做不到，而它们恰好都藏在两跳之外。这篇把整个功能讲一遍，按值得动手的顺序排，名字有误导性的地方会直接标出来。

数字健康 android 的控制项在哪

打开「设置」，找「数字健康与家长控制」。Pixel 上这一栏就在设置靠上的位置。三星手机上的名字一样，但这行可能排在「电池与设备维护」下面，多数人就是在这里找丢的。

不想翻菜单的话：打开设置，直接搜「数字健康」。我试过的各家界面都行得通，包括 One UI 和 MIUI，这两家会把菜单名改掉。

每一项工具实际做什么

工具它实际做什么值得设吗
数据面板今天的使用时长、解锁次数、收到的通知，按 App 分看就行，不用设
App 定时器给单个 App 按天设上限，到了就变灰值得，多数人就是为它来的
就寝模式按时间表让屏幕变灰、通知静音值得，四项里最有力的一项
专注模式暂停你选好的 App，直到你自己关掉，不按时间表值得，用在工作时段和吃饭时

问题出在命名上。四项里有两项叫「模式」，一项叫「定时器」，而最管用的那一项听起来像给小孩用的睡眠功能。

就寝模式与专注模式

两个都开的人，之后往往分不清是哪个把什么拦住了。它们对应的是不同的时刻。

  • 就寝模式按时间走。到你设的钟点自动开启，屏幕变灰、通知静音。用在睡前两小时。
  • 专注模式按你点一下走。先把会分心的 App 选好，之后它暂停这些 App，直到你关掉。用在工作时段或吃饭时。
  • 两者会叠加。同时开的时候就寝模式优先，这也是让人误以为专注模式坏了的原因。

screen time android 数据面板怎么看

数据面板是所有人都会看、几乎没人真读懂的部分。上面有三样东西比总时长有用得多：

  • 解锁次数。这是我最建议盯的数字。一次九十分钟，和四十次加起来九十分钟，不是同一种习惯，而总时长会把这一点藏起来。
  • 解锁后第一个打开的 App。Android 会显示它。这通常才是值得改的习惯，而且它很少是总时长最高的那个。
  • 收到的通知数。通知很多但使用时长一般，说明是手机在把你拉进去，而不是你主动打开。这是通知设置的问题，不是屏幕时间的问题。

android wellbeing 管不到的地方

有三条限制，建议先知道，再决定要不要把计划建在它上面。

App 定时器每天零点重置，也不能按工作日分别设。适合周三的上限放在周六就是错的，排班不固定的人没法用时间表绕过去。

定时器是按设备算的，不是按账号。同一款 App 在平板上有自己的定时器，所以这些数字永远加不出你真实花掉的时间。

浏览器是它彻底失效的地方。给 Chrome 设一个定时器，等于把本来分开设的所有东西塞进同一个上限，这个空档正是 pause-paw.shop 在补的：它跑在浏览器里、作用在标签页上，超过你设的滚动时长之后，下一个页面晚十秒加载。

今晚十分钟

  1. 把就寝模式设在睡前两小时。灰度别关。屏幕变得明显不好看，这正是它起作用的方式。
  2. 给「解锁后最常打开的那个 App」设一个定时器。一个就够，不要五个。
  3. 专注模式里放两个 App，先别开，需要的时候再开。
  4. 关掉三个最吵的 App 的通知，前提是它们不是来自真人的消息。

Every Android phone ships with android digital wellbeing built in, and most people open it once, look at a bar chart, and never come back. That is a waste, because four of the controls in there do things no third-party app can do, and they are the ones buried two taps deep. This is the whole feature set in the order worth setting up, with the parts that mislead named as they come up.

Where digital wellbeing android controls live

Open Settings, then Digital Wellbeing and parental controls. On a Pixel the entry sits near the top of Settings. On a Samsung phone it is in the same place by name, but the row can appear below Battery and device care, which is where most people lose it.

If you would rather not dig through menus, open Settings and type wellbeing into the search field. That works on every skin I have tried, including One UI and MIUI, where the menu labels get renamed.

What each tool actually does

ToolWhat it doesWorth setting up?
DashboardToday's screen time, unlock count and notifications, per appRead it, set nothing
App timersCaps one app per day, then greys it outYes, the one people come for
Bedtime modeGreys the screen and silences notifications on a scheduleYes, strongest of the four
Focus modePauses chosen apps until you turn it off, no scheduleYes, for work blocks and meals
Wind Down, on older phonesGrayscale plus Do Not Disturb on a scheduleSkip it, Bedtime mode replaced it

The naming is where people go wrong. Two of these are called a mode, one is called a timer, and the most effective one sounds like a sleep feature meant for a child.

Bedtime mode versus focus mode

People switch both on and then cannot tell which one blocked what. They are for different moments.

  • Bedtime mode runs on a clock. It starts at the hour you set, greys the screen and silences notifications. Use it for the last two hours before sleep.
  • Focus mode runs when you tap it. You pick the distracting apps once, then it pauses them until you switch it off. Use it for a work block or a meal.
  • They stack. With both active, Bedtime mode wins, which is what makes people think Focus mode has stopped working.

Reading the screen time android dashboard

The dashboard is the part everybody looks at and almost nobody reads properly. Three things on it tell you more than the total does:

  • Unlock count. This is the number I would watch. Ninety minutes in one session and ninety minutes across forty unlocks are not the same habit, and the total hides which one you have.
  • The app you open first after unlocking. Android shows it. It is usually the habit worth changing, and it is rarely the app with the biggest total.
  • Notifications received. High notification counts with moderate usage mean the phone is pulling you in rather than you choosing to open it. That is a notification setting problem, not a screen time android problem.

Where android wellbeing falls short

Three limits are worth knowing before you build a plan around it.

App timers reset at midnight and cannot be set per weekday. A limit that fits a Wednesday is wrong on a Saturday, and there is no way to schedule around a rotating shift.

Timers are per device, not per account. The same app on a tablet runs its own timer, so the numbers never add up to what you actually spent.

The browser is where it stops working at all. A timer on Chrome is one timer covering everything you would otherwise cap separately, which is the gap pause-paw.shop fills: it runs in the browser, on the tab, and after the scroll time you set the next page loads ten seconds later.

Ten minutes, tonight

  1. Set Bedtime mode for the two hours before sleep. Leave grayscale on. It makes the phone visibly less interesting, which is the point of it.
  2. Put a timer on the one app you open most after unlocking. One app, not five.
  3. Set Focus mode up with two apps in it and leave it switched off until you need it.
  4. Turn off notifications for the three noisiest apps that are not messages from actual people.
