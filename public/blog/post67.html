PausePaw Pro vs Family：哪个适合你

2026-10-07

同一套引擎，覆盖的人不一样。这篇按人数和机器数讲清 PausePaw 两个套餐各自适合谁。

会去比较 pausepaw pro vs family 的人，通常不是在比功能，而是在算清楚规则要覆盖几个人、几台机器。两个套餐跑的是同一套引擎，所以差别在覆盖范围：一个是给一个人做的，一个是给一屋子人做的。

PausePaw plans 说到底就是三个问题：谁的设备、多少条规则、谁能改。这三个答案出来，选择基本就定了。

两个套餐一样的地方

两边拿到的打断模型是一样的。一条规则盯着你在做什么，你一飘进名单里的东西，一只小动物就带着一个跳不过去的倒计时冒出来；倒计时结束，标签页安静几分钟。两个套餐都不给每日评分，也不给周报，因为它们本来就不是计量工具。

  • 倒计时：两边一样，而且都跳不过去。
  • 绑定任务的规则：跟着你手头在做什么走，不跟着钟点走。
  • 本地执行：规则跑在浏览器里，所以触发暂停不需要连服务器。

Pro 适合谁

Pro 是给一个人在自己机器上用的。规则条数比免费档多，而且能挂在「你在做什么」上，而不是挂在一个时间段上。如果你最糟的那一小时发生在下午的笔记本电脑上，Pro 就是完整的答案。

对多数人起决定作用的是两个细节。一是可以按站点设规则，而不是按类别，所以吃掉你下午的那四个标签页会被拦下，浏览器其余部分照常能用；二是暂停触发之后你可以改规则——这一点比听起来重要，因为修复就是改一条，不是发工单。

Family 适合谁

Family 是给不止一个人用的。规则不再只属于你自己，真正开始变得重要的问题是：谁能改它们。

  • 多个档案：一份订阅覆盖家里的人，而不是只覆盖其中一个。
  • 家长那一侧：给孩子设的屏幕时间规则，放在一个他没法悄悄改掉的地方。
  • 共享时段：作业时间和睡觉时间针对的是整个家，不是某一台机器。

代价是 Family 要求你真的去管它。如果家里没人会在第一周之后碰设置，那给真正要紧的那几台机器买 Pro 更简单。

五分钟内怎么选

先数人，再数机器。一个人用一两台机器，选 Pro。两个及以上的人，或者你想对孩子的屏幕时间有发言权，选 Family。

  • 选 Pro：问题出在你自己的下午、你自己的笔记本上。
  • 选 Family：你在给别人设规则，而且需要这些规则站得住。
  • 先用免费档：你还没拿一条规则去跑过自己最糟的那一小时。

常见问题

  • 以后能换套餐吗？能。已经写好的规则会带过去，换套餐的代价只有差价。
  • Family 管手机吗？PausePaw 跑在桌面浏览器里。手机需要另一个工具，两个套餐都一样。
  • 一个人住需要 Family 吗？不需要。Family 是为共享规则和家长侧做的，这两件事对一个人都没有意义。
  • 免费档够试吗？覆盖基本规则和提醒。等规则用完了再升级，不用提前。

Someone comparing pausepaw pro vs family is usually not comparing features. They are working out how many people and how many machines the rules have to cover. Both plans run the same engine, so the difference is reach: one is built for a person, the other for a household.

PausePaw plans come down to three questions. Whose devices, how many rules, and who gets to edit them. Once those three are answered, the choice is mostly made.

What the two plans share

Both get the same interruption model. A rule watches what you are doing, a small animal shows up with a countdown you cannot dismiss, and the tab goes quiet for a few minutes once the countdown ends. Neither plan adds a daily score or a weekly report, because neither plan is a measurement tool.

  • The countdown. Identical on both, and not dismissable on either.
  • Rules bound to a task. They follow what you are working on instead of a fixed hour on the clock.
  • Local enforcement. Rules run in the browser, so a pause fires without anything reaching a server.

Where Pro fits

Pro is the plan for one person on their own machines. You get more rules than the free tier, and you can attach them to what you are doing rather than to a time window. If your worst hour happens at the laptop in the afternoon, Pro is the whole answer.

Two details settle it for most people. Rules can be set per site instead of per category, so the four tabs that eat your afternoon get blocked while the rest of the browser keeps working. And you can edit a rule after a pause fires, which matters more than it sounds: the fix is one change, not a support ticket.

Where Family fits

Family is the plan for more than one person. The rules stop being yours alone, and the question that starts to matter is who can edit them.

  • Several profiles. One subscription covers the people in the house rather than one of them.
  • A parent side. Rules for a child's screen time live somewhere they cannot quietly undo.
  • Shared schedules. Homework hours and bedtime apply to the household, not to one machine.

The trade-off is that Family asks you to administer it. If nobody in the house is going to touch the settings after week one, Pro on the machines that matter is the simpler buy.

How to pick in five minutes

Count the people, then count the machines. One person on one or two machines goes Pro. Two or more people, or a child whose screen time you want a say in, goes Family.

  • Pick Pro if: the problem is your own afternoon, on your own laptop.
  • Pick Family if: you are setting rules for someone else and need them to hold.
  • Start free if: you have not yet run a rule through your worst hour.

FAQ

  • Can I switch plans later? Yes. Rules you already wrote carry over, so switching costs the price difference and nothing else.
  • Does Family cover phones? PausePaw runs in a desktop browser. Phones need a separate tool, on either plan.
  • Do I need Family if I live alone? No. Family exists for shared rule sets and a parent side, and neither does anything for one person.
  • Is the free tier enough to test this? It covers base rules and reminders. Upgrade once you run out of rules, not before.
